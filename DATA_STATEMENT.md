# Data Statement

The case identifiers are legal database citation references. Full judgment texts are not redistributed in this package.

The released case-level data are limited to:

- case reference identifiers;
- extracted first-instance and second-instance award amounts for the stress set;
- exact award-maintain/change labels;
- frame-membership flags used for the analysis;
- model raw responses and parsed numeric outputs.

The package is intended to permit numerical replication of the paper's tables and figures without republishing licensed judgment text or party-identifying factual narratives. Researchers who need to inspect the underlying judgments should retrieve them independently from the relevant legal database or public source using the case references.
